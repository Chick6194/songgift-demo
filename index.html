Questions

Good to know before you start

How long does delivery take?

Standard songs arrive in about 7 days, Rush in about 3 days. Premium with a music video takes about 10 days. Need it by a certain date? Add your deadline in the form.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Standard and Rush include one lyric revision; Premium includes two. Revisions cover small changes like wording or names.

How can I use my song?

Your song is for personal use: play it at the party, share it with family and post it to your own social media. Commercial use, resale or distribution requires a separate license.

Who sings the songs?

Real, professional artists from the TalentHub Online roster, not AI voices.
